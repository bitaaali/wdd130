Kiribati

Kiribati (pronounced Kee-ri-bahs) is an island nation in the central Pacific Ocean made up of 32 coral atolls and Banaba, spread across a massive expanse of ocean from the Gilbert Islands to the Line and Phoenix Islands. It gained independence from the United Kingdom on July 12, 1979, with its capital and government center in South Tarawa.
